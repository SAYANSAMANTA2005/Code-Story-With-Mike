Maternity leave in India is governed by the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (and related provisions under the Code on Social Security), which applies to establishments with 10 or more employees.

Eligibility

• Work Requirement: You must have worked for your employer for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding your expected date of delivery.
• Applicability: Covers women in factories, mines, plantations, shops, and corporate/private organizations.

Leave Duration

• First Two Children: 26 weeks of paid leave (up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery, and the remaining after).
• Third Child Onward: 12 weeks of paid leave (up to 6 weeks before delivery).
• Adoption or Surrogacy (Commissioning Mothers): 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over (applicable if the adopted child is under 3 months old).
• Miscarriage or Medical Termination: 6 weeks of paid leave following the day of the miscarriage.
• Tubectomy Operation: 2 weeks of paid leave following the procedure.

Financial & Monetary Benefits

• Paid Leave: Full average daily wage paid by the employer for the entire duration of the leave.
• Medical Bonus: A statutory medical bonus (typically ₹3,500 or as prescribed) if the employer does not provide free pre-natal and post-natal medical care.

Workplace Rights & Amenities

• Job Security: It is illegal for an employer to dismiss, discharge, or demote a woman during or due to maternity leave.
• Crèche Facility: Establishments with 50 or more employees must provide crèche (daycare) facilities, allowing the mother up to four daily visits.
• Nursing Breaks: Two daily nursing breaks during work hours until the child reaches 15 months of age.
