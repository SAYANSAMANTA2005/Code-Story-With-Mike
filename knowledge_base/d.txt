Choosing between GATE CSE (Computer Science and Engineering) and GATE DA (Data Science and Artificial Intelligence) depends on your academic background, career goals, and preferred subjects.

Syllabus Comparison

• GATE CSE: Focuses on core computer systems and theory. Subjects include Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Computer Organization & Architecture, Theory of Computation, Compiler Design, Digital Logic, DBMS, Algorithms, Data Structures, and basic Mathematics/Aptitude.
• GATE DA: Focuses heavily on mathematics, machine learning, and data handling. Subjects include Probability & Statistics, Linear Algebra, Calculus & Optimization, Python Programming, Data Structures, DBMS & Data Warehousing, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. It omits heavy core systems subjects like OS, Computer Networks, COA, and TOC.
• Overlap: Roughly 60% to 65% of the syllabus is shared between both papers (including general aptitude, basic programming/data structures, DBMS, and foundational linear algebra/probability).
