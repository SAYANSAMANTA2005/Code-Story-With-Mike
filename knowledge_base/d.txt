In the last video, we learned how text becomes a vector and how to compare two vectors with cosine similarity. In this video, we use those two ingredients on real documents.
We create a small fake company knowledge base, load the documents, embed each document, and build a search function that finds the best matching documents for a question. Then we deliberately expose the problems of this simple first version: one embedding per document is blurry because of pooling, everything is re-embedded on every start, too much text would go to the LLM, and the best score always exists even when no document answers the question. Each problem is fixed in the upcoming videos. Our agent stays untouched in this video.
